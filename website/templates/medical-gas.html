Medical Gas Supply Units (BHU & Pendants)

Wall-mounted bed head units and ceiling pendants that keep medical gases, power, data, and lighting organized right at the patient bed or procedure table.

What We Do

Clean, accessible medical gas connections where care happens.

Doctors and nurses need immediate, clutter-free access to oxygen, suction, and medical air during patient care and procedures. Bed Head Units (BHUs) and ceiling pendants bring those outlets together in a neat, ergonomic layout.

Centerline plans, supplies, installs, tests, commissions, and supports medical gas supply units for wards, ICUs, recovery bays, and treatment rooms.

How It Works

Gas Outlets, Pendants & Bedside Utilities

Medical Gas Supply Outlets

Configured for key clinical gases including Oxygen (O₂), Vacuum, Medical Air, Nitrogen (N₂), Nitrous Oxide (N₂O), AGSS, and CO₂.

Bed Head Units (BHUs)

Wall-mounted horizontal or vertical units for patient rooms and ICUs, keeping gas outlets, nurse call/data, and lighting neatly aligned.

Ceiling Medical Pendants

Articulated overhead arms that lift equipment and gas outlets off the floor for flexible positioning around beds and procedure tables.

Power, Data & Task Lighting

Integrates AC electrical sockets, monitoring/data connections, and patient or exam lighting into one coordinated point-of-care unit.
